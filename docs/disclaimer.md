# What DocuMDR is not

DocuMDR drafts software-lifecycle records from annotations in a repository you choose. The drafts are for your own review.

- It is not a Notified Body and it does not issue a CE mark.
- It is not legal, regulatory, or clinical advice.
- It does not declare conformity with EU MDR, IEC 62304, ISO 14971, ISO 13485, or the EU AI Act.
- It does not decide EU MDR class. A Rule 11 note is a non-binding record of what a person stated.
- It does not estimate risk probability or severity, and it does not accept residual risk.
- It does not file vigilance or EU AI Act Article 73 reports.
- An approval gate is an internal review record. It is not an eIDAS qualified electronic signature and it is not a signature on the EU declaration of conformity.
- Using the tool does not, by itself, create a quality management system.

A qualified person on the manufacturer's team has to review, correct, and approve the underlying technical documentation. Harmonised standards and their presumption of conformity depend on Commission decisions and are not hardcoded here.

IEC 62304 and ISO 14971 are copyrighted standards. This repository implements a workflow informed by public regulatory text and by published descriptions of those standards. It does not copy the standards.
