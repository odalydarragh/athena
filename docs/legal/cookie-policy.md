# Cookie Policy — DocuMDR

**Effective date:** 6 October 2026

DocuMDR uses only **strictly necessary** cookies to keep you signed in. Under S.I. 336/2011 (ePrivacy), strictly necessary cookies do not require consent.

| Cookie | Type | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|------|---------|----------|
| `documdr_session` | Strictly necessary | Authenticated session (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax; Secure in production) | Session, or 7 days if “keep me signed in” is offered |

We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or social-media cookies in this version. No third-party cookies are set.

You can delete cookies in your browser; you will be signed out.
